The Experience

We stay out of the way, so the real moments can happen.

A wedding is hundreds of small, unrepeatable moments happening at once. Our job is to be present for them without becoming part of them — no interruptions, no directing, no asking anyone to do it again.

The result is an album that feels like the day itself: your mother adjusting your dupatta, the cousin who cries first, the silence right after the pheras.

How It Works

From first hello to final album.

Four calm steps, so you can enjoy your wedding while we take care of the rest.

  1. 01

    The conversation

    A relaxed call or coffee to understand your families, functions and the moments you’re afraid of missing.

  2. 02

    Planning & recce

    We map the timeline, visit key venues where possible and plan light, positions and quiet backup routes.

  3. 03

    The wedding days

    We arrive early, stay low-profile and cover every function — from the first haldi to the vidaai.

  4. 04

    Delivery

    A private preview gallery first, then your hand-edited album and film, delivered with care.

Good To Know

Questions, answered.

How early should we book?

Popular dates in peak season fill months ahead, so share your date as soon as it’s fixed. We will confirm availability within a day.

Do you travel outside Delhi?

Yes. We cover weddings across India and abroad. Travel and stay are included in a single, transparent quote.

How long until we receive our photographs and film?

You receive a private preview gallery first, followed by the full hand-edited gallery and film. Exact timelines are agreed in your collection.

Can we customise a collection?

Every collection is tailored after a conversation. Add, remove or extend coverage so it fits your functions and budget.

Do you stage poses or reshoot moments?

No. We photograph moments as they happen and only guide gently during portraits, so the album stays honest.
